# Support guidance

This is a candidate organization default requiring explicit maintainer adoption.
Repository-specific support and security policies take precedence.

For a non-sensitive question or reproducible bug, use the affected repository's
available issue templates. Include its exact source/version, a minimal synthetic
example, expected and observed behavior, and the limited checks already tried.
Describe configuration with placeholders instead of operator files or endpoints.

Public reports should be in English and omit personal identities, workstation
paths, credentials, wallet/signing data, transaction/account inventories and raw
private logs. A security-sensitive issue belongs in the repository's verified
private reporting channel. Do not turn a missing support route into public
disclosure or direct a report to an unrelated product repository.

Maintainers must state the supported versions/environments and response ownership.
This candidate promises no service availability, support deadline, recovery of
funds, bounty or production readiness. A devnet example or passing CI result is
not a support or release commitment.
